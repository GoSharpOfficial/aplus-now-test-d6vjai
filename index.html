How to install

  1. Open this page on your Android phone (Android 8 or newer).
  2. Tap Download APK. If your browser asks, allow the download.
  3. If Android says it can't install unknown apps, tap Settings and switch on Allow from this source for your browser, then go back.
  4. Open the downloaded file and tap Install.
  5. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details, then Install anyway. This is a private test build and isn't on the Play Store.

What to try

  • Create an account (or sign in), then verify with the in-app Demo Mailbox.
  • Tap Request a Service and complete the three steps.
  • Check the notification that arrives and the status timeline under Requests.
  • Go to Profile > Settings > Demo Tools, tap To Admin, change the request's status, then switch back with To Client.
